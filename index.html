01 Our story

It started in a garage, with a 1kg roaster and a lot of burnt beans.

A roast lives or dies in the last thirty seconds before the drop. We built the whole bar around that same idea: every pour, every shot, every pass of the steam wand is a small decision made on purpose.

The notebook full of failed batches from 2016 still sits behind the counter, next to the roaster we run by hand three mornings a week.

  • No.1 No syrup pumps. Ever.
  • No.2 The house blend doesn't change without a sign on the door.
  • No.3 If you ask what's pouring, we'll let you taste it.
